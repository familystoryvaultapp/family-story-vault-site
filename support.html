Support

Help with Family Story Vault.

Contact support

Questions about Family Story Vault can be sent to our support team.

Email
familystoryvaultapp@gmail.com

When reporting a problem

Please include what you were trying to do, what happened instead, your device model and iOS version, and—if possible—a screenshot of the issue. Please do not send private family recordings, backup files, or sensitive family information unless support specifically asks for information needed to diagnose the problem.

Protect important memories

Family Story Vault is local-first. Create Complete Vault Backups regularly and keep valid copies somewhere safe outside the app. Removing the app, clearing its data, or losing or resetting the device may cause data loss when no valid external backup exists.

Privacy and terms

For details about local storage, exports, sharing, and your responsibilities, read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
