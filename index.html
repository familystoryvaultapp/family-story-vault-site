Your family is not a product

Preservation should start with trust.

The early Family Story Vault experience is being built local-first, with your family content stored on your device and exportable by you. We are intentionally avoiding the engagement mechanics of social media.

  • No public follower counts
  • No advertising feed
  • No AI impersonation of deceased relatives
  • Backup and portability are core product goals
Read our current privacy approach →
